# Habit Tracker M3E v1.2.5

Experience the most refined, fluid, and responsive version of **Habit Tracker M3E** yet.

## What's New in v1.2.5

### 🎬 Cinematic Splash Launch Experience
- **Fluid Video Intro:** Replaced the static splash screen with a cinematic intro animation (`assets/splash_animation.mp4`) that plays smoothly on app launch.
- **Butter-Smooth Transition:** Seamless cross-fade and scale reveal from the splash video into the app dashboard.

### 🧈 Refined Screen Transitions
- **iOS-style Fluid Navigation:** Enhanced navigation across all Settings sections with silky-smooth horizontal parallax transitions, natural deceleration curves, and interactive swipe-to-go-back gestures.

### 📏 Pixel-Perfect Docks & Switchers
- **Zero Overflow Layout:** Completely eliminated layout overflow warnings across all segmented switches, theme selectors, check style pickers, and preference sliders.
- **Responsive Geometry:** Automatically adapts cleanly to all screen sizes and densities.

### 🚀 ABI-Aware Updates & Optimized APKs
- **Architecture Specific APKs:** Download smaller, optimized APKs tailored directly for your device architecture:
  - `arm64-v8a` (Modern 64-bit Android devices)
  - `armeabi-v7a` (32-bit Android devices)
  - `x86_64` (64-bit Emulators & Intel/AMD Android devices)
  - `Universal` (Compatible with all devices)
- **Automatic Architecture Detection:** In-app updates now auto-detect device ABI and download the exact matching APK seamlessly.
