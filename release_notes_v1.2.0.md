# Habit Tracker M3E — v1.2.0

We are excited to bring you **v1.2.0**, packed with fluid UI animations, interactive dock controls, profile verification badges, expanded app icon options, and a refined in-app update experience.

---

### ✨ What's New in v1.2.0

#### 🎛️ Interactive Sliding Dock Controls
* **Animated Dock Selectors**: Experience a completely overhauled settings interface with physics-inspired sliding dock controls.
* **Theme & Check Style Switcher**: Seamlessly transition between System, Light, and Dark themes or toggle Square and Circle checkmark styles with smooth sliding indicator animations and tactile haptic feedback.
* **Streamlined Preferences**: All 18 habit and privacy preferences—including *Activity on cards*, *View switcher*, *Connect the days*, *Last 12 months*, *Focus mode*, *Notes*, *To-Do*, *Gamification*, *Plan the day*, *Swipe habit cards*, *Progress on Today*, and *App Lock*—now feature modern animated sliding dock toggles.

#### 🎨 Expanded Icon Pack with Interactive Icon Dock
* **New Orange App Icon**: In addition to *Default*, *Green*, and *Tricolor*, an all-new *Orange* app icon variant is now available.
* **Swipe-to-Reveal Dock**: The Appearance settings icon picker features a horizontally scrollable dock selector—simply slide to discover and activate the Orange theme.
* Full support for adaptive launcher icons on Android.

#### 🛡️ Verified Profile Badges
* **Get Verified**: Customize your personal profile with an authentic rosette verified badge inspired by modern social platforms.
* **Three Color Variants**: Choose from *Sky Blue*, *Black*, and *Violet* via an animated switcher, or keep it clean and badge-free.
* High-precision scalloped geometry rendered crisply beside your profile name.

#### 🌊 Fluid In-App Updates System
* **Fluid Water Wave Animation**: When a new version is detected, the update card comes to life with a fluid 3-second sinusoidal wave ripple animation.
* **Precise Download Size**: Updates now clearly display the exact download package size in megabytes (MB).
* Direct, reliable APK download and one-click package installation directly within the app.

#### 💡 Feature Requests & Community Support
* **Request New Features**: Added a direct feature request portal in the Support section, allowing you to suggest new capabilities and enhancements on GitHub.
* Enhanced issue reporting and share links.

#### 🔒 Privacy & Core Philosophy
* **100% Local & Offline**: Your habits, streaks, and data remain entirely on your device. Zero ads, zero paywalls, zero telemetry.
