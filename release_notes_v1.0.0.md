# 🚀 Welcome to Habit Tracker v1.0.0

A modern, minimal, private, and ad-free habit tracking application designed to help you build consistency, sustain discipline, and reach your personal milestones. With an offline-first architecture, customizable Material 3 Expressive aesthetics, deep analytics, and integrated focus tools, Habit Tracker puts you in complete control of your daily routine.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Capabilities

### 📊 Intelligent Habit Tracking
* **Multiple Habit Types:**
  * **Normal Habits:** Build positive daily practices with single-tap check-ins.
  * **Avoid Habits:** Break unwanted routines and monitor relapses with resilience tracking.
  * **Amount Habits:** Quantify measurable goals with custom units (e.g., liters of water, book pages, workout minutes, or repetitions).
* **Sub-task Checklists:** Break down multi-step routines into intuitive subtasks that mark the habit complete once all steps are checked.
* **Flexible Schedules:** Tailor habits to your schedule—choose daily tracking, specific days of the week, custom intervals (every *N* days), or flexible weekly/monthly targets.
* **Retroactive Logging & Day Notes:** Seamlessly back-fill missed days or long-press any past date to record reflections and attach photo notes.
* **Vacation Mode:** Pause habits without losing streaks or breaking consistency metrics while traveling or taking time off.

---

### 📈 Visual Progress & Deep Analytics
* **Interactive Heatmap Grids:** Visualize your consistency over time with GitHub-style activity matrices viewable across weekly, monthly, and full-year perspectives.
* **Calendar & Trend Overviews:** Explore monthly completion breakdowns, compare your current streak against your all-time best, and evaluate consistency trends.
* **Habit Strength Scoring:** Advanced algorithmic strength calculation that reflects long-term dedication rather than a single missed day.
* **Time-of-Day Insights:** Analyze the exact hours and days you are most productive to optimize your schedule.
* **Exportable Progress Cards:** Generate high-resolution, beautifully styled accomplishment cards to document and share your journey.

---

### ⏱️ Integrated Focus & Pomodoro Sessions
* **Linked Habit Timers:** Connect focus sessions directly to your habits and track total time spent toward your goals.
* **Pomodoro & Free Flow:** Choose between structured Pomodoro cycles (work sessions with configurable short/long breaks) or continuous stopwatch focus.
* **Ambient Soundscapes:** Immerse yourself in built-in calming audio, including gentle rain, crackling fire, soothing brown noise, and customizable soundscapes.
* **Aesthetic Displays:** Switch between clean Circle Progress, retro Flip Clock, and modern Digital Dot interfaces, complete with keep-screen-awake support.

---

### 🎨 Material 3 Expressive Design & Personalization
* **Three Design Styles:** Switch seamlessly between **Classic**, **Minimal**, and **Expressive** interfaces, each featuring distinct typography, shapes, and motion physics.
* **Dynamic Material You & AMOLED:** Full dynamic color theming that adapts to your wallpaper, complete with a manual color picker and pure-black AMOLED dark mode for battery savings.
* **Custom Visual Accents:** Select from custom icon packs, curated emojis, cover images, custom categories, and drag-and-drop habit sorting.
* **Celebration Effects:** Rewarding confetti bursts upon completing all daily habits.

---

### 📱 Home-Screen Widgets & Smart Notifications
* **Interactive Android Widgets:** Log completions instantly right from your home screen without launching the full app. Available in Habit, Today Overview, Statistics, and Activity Heatmap formats.
* **Actionable Notifications:** Check off habits, snooze reminders, or log amount increments directly from the system notification shade.

---

### 🔒 Privacy-First Architecture & Data Control
* **100% Offline & Private:** Zero account registration, zero cloud tracking, and zero telemetry. All your data stays securely on your device.
* **Comprehensive Backups:** Export and restore your complete database with encrypted or plain JSON files, plus scheduled local auto-backups.
* **Seamless Migration:** Built-in import tools supporting Loop Habit Tracker, HabitKit, Habitica, HabitBull, and generic CSV formats.
* **Biometric App Lock:** Safeguard your personal journal entries and habit logs with fingerprint or PIN protection.
* **Over-the-Air Updates:** In-app updates dashboard to check for, download, and install new versions directly.
